Introduction

We are introducing a unique Tier Rank System built to reward pure skill and consistency — not purchases. Unlike typical servers, every rank here is earned through gameplay. Gear up in-game, prepare yourself, and step into a system where only performance matters.

How It Works

• Gear up and prepare your loadout before entering ranked combat
• Players compete in Rank Matches every Saturday & Sunday
• Matches take place directly in the server
• Winning grants Rank Points (ELO-based system)
• Accumulate points and climb through tiers
• 100% skill-based progression — no shortcuts, no pay-to-win
